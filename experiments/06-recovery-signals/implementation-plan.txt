BONSAI RECOVERY SIGNALS: IMPLEMENTATION PLAN
REVISED EVALUATION REQUIREMENTS (user steering, 2026-10-03)
- Exclude wall-clock deadlines, latency, throughput, and matched-time scores from
  model performance evaluation. Use common prespecified token/action budgets and
  report budget-conditioned partial performance. Operational Slurm limits require
  checkpoint/resume or an explicitly incomplete trial, never a model failure.
- Require tested context compaction and continuation with the task environment
  preserved. Use exact server tokenization, reserve output/summary space, save raw
  history outside active context, and provide retrieval. A context-management
  infrastructure failure is an invalid/incomplete trial until repaired/resumed.
- Original archived configs already enabled summarization. Music and Vigenere
  logs show overflow recovery, failed full summaries, short-summary attempts, and
  cancellation. Exact failure cause is not established. Do not describe these
  as clean failures caused solely by a model's finite context capacity.
- Primary proposed partial score: weighted, independently verified requirement
  satisfaction per task; freeze the rubric before model unblinding. Record initial
  score, final score, net improvement, and regressions. Average tasks equally.
- Complement with a stronger, independent, reference-informed process judge given
  audited specification, reference solution, checks, and actual trajectory evidence.
  Separate factual correctness, decision justification given available evidence,
  and observed execution results. Score observable reasoning, not hidden cognition.
  Reference solutions are examples, not unique required implementation paths.
- Calibrate judge labels against independently checked cases and adjudicate
  disagreements before treating scores as RL rewards. Do not make Qwen the sole
  judge of itself versus Bonsai. Keep offline privileged evaluator information
  separate from the agent and from the common neutral compaction helper.
- Keep pass/fail as a secondary endpoint; report partial outcome and process scores
  separately with explicit definitions. No new scores have yet been computed under
  this proposed rubric. These would be custom diagnostics, not official TB scores.

RECOMMENDATION

Use quantization-aware on-policy distillation as the first eventual training
baseline, after a separately validated quantizer exists. Treat a Qwen process
judge as an experimentally calibrated auxiliary signal, not an objective oracle.
Retain executable outcome checks as the criterion for actual improvement.

This recommendation combines two established ideas, not a demonstrated result
for Bonsai: quantization-aware distillation recovers compressed-model behavior,
and on-policy distillation addresses mismatched training/deployment histories.
Whether their combination repairs this model's long-horizon behavior remains an
empirical question. Low precision is not the only difference between released
Bonsai and the current downloaded parent; provenance and post-training matter.

WHY NOT START WITH AN UNCALIBRATED REASONING JUDGE?

The September 29 music study found explicit premise revision in 22/40 Bonsai
versus 39/40 FP16 samples, but useful next actions in 38/40 versus 39/40. A reward
for saying the right thing could amplify a large textual difference with little
behavioral benefit. Reasoning text is observable output, not privileged access
to the causal internal reasoning of either model.

The original task comparison also contains shared errors: both models missed the
same four MVCC checks, and both failed the session verifier. Bonsai did better on
React business-calendar behavior while Qwen did better on other requirements.
Teacher agreement is therefore not synonymous with task improvement.

The October 3 verifier audit adds a concrete warning about reward coverage: on a
fresh task image, the benchmark reference passes all 7 official tests but only
15/18 extra event-order conservation checks. Qwen's saved artifact passes 18/18
conservation checks but only 3/7 official tests, as does Bonsai on official tests.
Keep the two endpoints separate; neither reference agreement nor this small test
suite establishes full correctness. The positive control also demonstrates that
the extra conservation endpoint is achievable by an existing implementation.

The catastrophic music repetition is a separate output-degeneration candidate.
There is only one observed event; do not estimate its frequency or attribute it
to growing quantization noise from that one trace. Measure it with repeated
samples and bounded output, separately from semantic recovery.

On-policy distillation does not automatically make a teacher recover. Given a
student's mistaken reasoning prefix, the teacher may continue the same mistake.
Test its recovery policy at those prefixes before treating it as a target. The
teacher may also assign high probability to repetition once a repetitive suffix
is established. Prevention at the initiating decision can matter more than
matching distributions after the loop is entrenched.

ESTABLISHING A LONG-HORIZON EFFECT

A rising KL curve is neither necessary nor sufficient. Even a constant small
extra error probability at each decision can reduce success over many decisions.
Conversely, KL can increase because later text is harder, without causing failure.
Long failed runs are selected survivors and may be long because they already
failed; total realized length is not an independent task-difficulty variable.

Use matched tasks with independently specified horizon (for example, K composed
state transitions with checked invariants), matched token and tool budgets, and
multiple seeds. Separately vary context length at the SAME decision state using
controlled history retention/compression that preserves necessary evidence.
Measure first verified harmful action, recovery conditional on a shared error,
and the interaction between model and prescribed horizon/context length. Include
within-model repeats and teacher/teacher baselines. The present study tests local
recovery only; it cannot establish that interaction by itself.

IMPORTANT LIMITS OF THIS STUDY

The music and session states are selected after inspecting failures. Repeated
seeds estimate variability at these states, not generalization across tasks.
The session snapshots are final artifacts plus a new review request, not original
intermediate containers. Music is reconstructed by replaying archived commands
with pinned dependencies; the runtime now includes tmux and preinstalled Pillow.
Both models have matched conditions, but this is not a bit-exact original rerun.

Current continuations follow the generating model. A better outcome can reflect
its later choices, not only the selected initial action. Judge/outcome associations
are diagnostic and do not establish the causal value of an individual action.
The tests are held out of the agent conversation, but are benchmark development
diagnostics. They must not enter a future training corpus.

NEXT VALIDATION BEFORE ANY RL REWARD

Build a small collection of independently authored environments with a verified
solution and controllable failures: state-machine invariants, stale assumptions
after API errors, schema migration, and code changes requiring regression tests.
Split by entire repository/task family and generator template before collecting
training data; a different random seed of one template is not a strong test set.

For each frozen environment snapshot s:
  - Generate candidate actions from both models, including alternatives from the
    same model. Preserve action and reasoning separately.
  - Fork the same snapshot for every candidate, execute its action, then continue
    every branch with the SAME frozen rollout policy and remaining budget.
  - Repeat continuations. Estimate Q(s,a) as mean externally verified return.
  - Estimate V(s) using a fixed baseline policy over actions at the same state.
  - Use Q(s,a)-V(s) as an empirical advantage estimate with uncertainty, not a
    single arbitrary judge score. This target is conditional on the chosen
    continuation policy and budget; record both.
  - Compare blinded judge scores, teacher likelihood, and random/length baselines
    for ranking actions within a state. Test rank correlation and calibration on
    held-out states/families. Bootstrap by independent state, not by token.

Use both groundedness and outcome prediction in the judge rubric. An action can
be justified yet fail through stochastic execution; conversely a false premise
can accidentally lead to a useful inspection. Do not collapse these labels.
Include deliberately equivalent commands with different explanations to detect
style bias. Include test execution, parser validity, exceptions, regressions, and
task completion as separate labels. An adversarially broken verifier should be
a verifier failure, never an automatic learning target.

WHAT THE EVENTUAL DISTILLATION TRAINER WOULD DO

Prerequisites owned by the separate quantization project:
  - pinned full-precision parent, tokenizer, template, and reasoning format;
  - fake-quantized differentiable student with known scale/rounding/rotation rules;
  - tested forward parity between that student and its exported deployment model;
  - optimizer/shadow-weight state and a reproducible initial checkpoint;
  - measured memory and throughput for the required context lengths.

Sampling loop:
  1. Sample an independent training environment and start/reset state.
  2. Roll out the current quantized student. Execute tools in isolated containers.
  3. Save the exact model input tokens, output tokens, reasoning boundaries,
     tool-call spans, environment observations, stop reason, RNG seed, and model
     revision. Keep tool results as conditioning context, not model targets.
  4. Teacher-force the SAME prefixes through frozen full-precision Qwen and through
     the fake-quantized student. Compute masked distillation loss on model-owned
     output positions. Do not include future outputs in a prefix.
  5. Update student shadow parameters through the quantized forward pass, using a
     validated straight-through or other quantizer gradient estimator.
  6. Export and verify the resulting quantized student; refresh on-policy rollouts.
     Reusing old rollouts indefinitely becomes off-policy data reuse.

A simple first loss is forward KL:
  L_KD = mean over selected output positions of
         sum_v p_teacher(v|prefix) * log[p_teacher(v|prefix)/p_student(v|prefix)].

Detach generated trajectories and teacher outputs. This direct distillation loss
does not differentiate through the student's sampling process; it is not an RL
policy-gradient estimator. GKD supports this style of update on student states.
Forward/reverse KL and JSD are different objectives; select explicitly and test,
do not call all of them interchangeable 'per-token KL rewards'.

A sampled token log-ratio log p_teacher(y_t)-log p_student(y_t) is a possible
negative reverse-KL reward estimator when sampled from the appropriate student
distribution. Optimizing a sequence objective with it requires correct policy
gradient, credit assignment, old-policy accounting, and length/variance controls.
Do not simply backpropagate through cached scalar log-ratios and call that RL.
Sampling truncation (top-k/top-p/min-p) changes the behavior distribution and must
be accounted for if treating samples as estimators of an untruncated objective.

Short implementation skeleton, intentionally not executable training code:
  rollout = run_environment(quantized_student, training_task)
  tokens, target_mask = exact_replay_tokens(rollout)
  teacher_logits = frozen_teacher(tokens)
  student_logits = fake_quant_student(tokens)
  loss = masked_forward_kl(teacher_logits, student_logits, target_mask)
  optimizer_step(loss)
  export_and_validate(student)

Masking and weighting:
- Exclude user prompts and tool/environment output from the loss.
- Include stop/format tokens so malformed or runaway output is not ignored.
- Preserve causal history and recurrent/cache semantics. Chunking a hybrid model
  with reset hidden state is a different computation, not memory optimization.
- Test separate weights for reasoning and command spans. Do not let a 200k-token
  reasoning/repetition episode dominate all useful tool decisions by token count.
- Per-action normalization changes the objective; document it and compare with
  ordinary token averaging. Confidence weights need validation: low teacher
  entropy can reflect confident error.
- Mix broad clean preservation data with student-visited recovery states to avoid
  spending the whole update on a handful of abnormal histories. Compare at least
  teacher-only versus mixed/on-policy data at the same compute budget.

COMPUTE/LOGGING DESIGN

Do not save all-vocabulary distributions for every token of a long trajectory as
JSON. With vocabulary 248,320, 32,768 positions alone take about 16.3 GB per model
in FP16 logits, before gradients or activations. Stream/chunk logits and loss
reduction while preserving the model's correct causal computation.

A teacher top-k plus residual bucket can be a defined approximation, but dropping
and renormalizing the tail silently changes KL. Record retained mass and validate
against exact KL on small batches. Neither a GGUF API's sampled-token logprobs nor
our current one-boundary measurements supply a full distillation training target.

On voodoo, all data generation, evaluation, scoring, and future training jobs use
Slurm and Docker. In this diagnostic phase, two 96 GB GPUs each serve one model,
with four slots/model. Forty CPUs and 220 GB host RAM are allocated; the user
allowed up to four GPUs and 80 CPUs for this study. Job-specific containers are
removed on exit/cancellation. Never reuse unrelated containers or cancel others'
jobs to obtain capacity. Record image IDs, model hashes, and Slurm IDs.

RELATED PRIMARY SOURCES

GKD: On-policy Distillation of Language Models: Learning from Self-Generated
Mistakes. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13649
Relevant: direct distillation on student-generated prefixes, distinct from the
sequence-level policy-gradient treatment used by MiniLLM.

MiniLLM. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08543
Relevant: reverse-KL sequence distillation and its optimization details; not a
license to identify every token's teacher disagreement with causal error.

Quantization-Aware Distillation for NVFP4 Inference Accuracy Recovery.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20088
Relevant: evidence for teacher KL supervision in quantized post-trained models.
Different format/models: not evidence that ternary Bonsai recovery is solved.

What Makes Low-Bit Quantization-Aware Training Work for Reasoning LLMs?
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14888
Relevant: distillation objectives and PTQ initialization; task/domain alignment
matters. Does not establish the proposed long-horizon failure mechanism here.

Process Reward Models for LLM Agents: Practical Framework and Directions.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.10325
Relevant: rollout-derived action values and agent process rewards. Different
environments; not direct validation of Qwen as a Bonsai judge.

The Lessons of Developing Process Reward Models in Mathematical Reasoning.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07301
Relevant: step-label quality and reward-model evaluation pitfalls. Math evidence
should not be assumed to transfer unchanged to executable agent workflows.
